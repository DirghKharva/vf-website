Our Story

Cultivating Goodness,
Exporting Excellence

VF Agri Export was founded with a clear purpose: to connect India's exceptional agricultural produce with buyers around the world. What started as a small venture with a handful of vegetable exports has grown into a trusted B2B export partner for buyers across 20+ countries.

We specialize in fresh vegetables, fresh fruits, and premium salt — three categories where we have built deep farm relationships, strong quality systems, and reliable export logistics.

Our approach is straightforward: work directly with farmers, inspect rigorously, package carefully, and deliver on time. No compromises. Every pallet that ships from our facility carries the trust of every farmer who grew it.

Over the years, we've earned a reputation for consistency, transparency, and the kind of quality that makes buyers come back season after season.
