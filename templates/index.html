About VF Agri Export

Cultivating Excellence,
Delivering Quality

VF Agri Export is a trusted name in the agricultural export industry, committed to bridging India's finest farms with global markets. We specialize in premium fresh vegetables, fruits, and salt — all sourced from carefully vetted, sustainable farms.

Our journey began with a simple belief: the world deserves the best of what India grows. With over a decade of experience in agricultural trade, we have built lasting relationships with buyers across 20+ countries, always delivering on our promise of quality and reliability.

Every product that leaves our facility meets strict quality standards — from farm inspection to packaging and export documentation. We are more than an exporter; we are your trusted agricultural partner.
